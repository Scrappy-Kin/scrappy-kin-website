1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Scrappy Kin ("we", "us", "our") handles your information.

Core Principle: We don't want your data. We built Scrappy Kin so that we *can't* see it.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We designed the App to be "Zero-Trust". This means:

  • No PII Collection: We do not collect or store your name, address, or ID on our servers. That data lives only on your device.
  • No Request Logging: We do not know which brokers you have opted out of.
  • No Email Access: When you connect your email, the App accesses it locally on your device to find broker receipts. We do not have remote access to your inbox.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Anonymous Telemetry (Opt-In)

If you explicitly opt in, the App may share:

  • Broker Behavior Data: Which brokers are compliant, which are broken, and which are stalling. This data is anonymized and does not contain your personal info.
  • App Health: Crash reports or bugs (manually submitted by you).

3.2 Subscription Data

If you purchase a subscription:

  • Transactions are processed by Apple (App Store). We receive a receipt to validate your subscription, but we do not receive your credit card details or billing address.

4. Where Your Data Lives

  • Personal Profile: On your device.
  • Opt-Out History: On your device.
  • Email Credentials: On your device (using standard OAuth tokens).

5. Security

Because we do not store your data, there is no central database of user profiles to hack. Your privacy security depends on the security of your own device (passcode, biometrics).

6. Open Source

Our code is open source. You (or security researchers) can verify these claims by inspecting the codebase.
