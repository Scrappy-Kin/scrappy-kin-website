Scrappy Kin Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026
Applies to: The Scrappy Kin iPhone app, Gmail sending, and user-initiated support conversations.

This policy explains what stays on your iPhone, what goes through Google when you send an email, and what Scrappy Kin receives only if you choose to contact us. Scrappy Kin requests only Google's gmail.send permission and does not operate an account or user-data backend.

1. Who we are

Scrappy Kin Inc. ("Scrappy Kin", "we", "us") is a Canadian company that builds tools for sending data-broker opt-out requests without handing Scrappy Kin access to your inbox.

2. What this policy covers

  • The Scrappy Kin iPhone app
  • Scrappy Kin's use of the Gmail API permission gmail.send
  • Support or privacy emails you choose to send to Scrappy Kin

4. Data we do not access

  • We do not read, index, or store your Gmail inbox, labels, or metadata.
  • We do not request Gmail permissions that would let Scrappy Kin read your email, inspect message metadata, or change your mailbox.
  • We do not sell personal data or use Google data for advertising.
  • We do not run background services that send email without your review and approval.

5. How Gmail data flows

  1. You tap Connect Gmail and Google shows the OAuth consent screen.
  2. Scrappy Kin requests only gmail.send so the app can deliver the prepared broker emails you review and approve.
  3. After you approve the permission, Scrappy Kin stores it in the iPhone Keychain and uses it only when you approve a send.
  4. No Gmail content is copied to Scrappy Kin servers. The email goes directly from your iPhone to Google, then to the broker or privacy contact shown in the prepared request.
  5. When you disconnect Gmail inside Scrappy Kin, the app asks Google to revoke access and deletes the saved permission from your iPhone.
  6. If you remove access in Google Account settings, Google disables the permission but cannot delete data stored on your iPhone. Use Scrappy Kin's disconnect control if you also want to remove the local copy.

6. Local storage and diagnostics

  • The saved Gmail permission lives only in the iPhone Keychain. Scrappy Kin does not sync it to our servers.
  • Your profile, email wording, send queue, and sent history stay in encrypted local app storage on your iPhone.
  • Deleting all local data removes that local app data and disconnects Gmail. Deleting local data does not cancel an Apple subscription.
  • Scrappy Kin uses no analytics SDKs, no third-party tracking tools, and no automatic crash-reporting services.
  • Optional local diagnostics are off by default; if you turn them on, logs stay on your device and are only shared if you manually export and send them to us.
  • Troubleshooting logs record limited app events and state, not Gmail content.

7. Limited Use compliance

Scrappy Kin complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Gmail data is used only to send the opt-out emails you initiate and to show you a local record of the attempt.
  • Gmail data is transferred only to Google as the email provider and to the broker or privacy contact shown in the prepared email you approve.
  • No Gmail data is used for advertising, human training, or unrelated features.
  • Human access to Google user data occurs only when you explicitly share details with Scrappy Kin support.

8. How we share data

We only share Gmail data in these cases:

  • With Google, solely to send the email you approved via the Gmail API
  • With the broker or privacy contact shown in the prepared email you approve

Because Scrappy Kin keeps Gmail content, the saved Gmail permission, and your opt-out data on your iPhone, the main user-specific information we may hold is whatever you directly send us through support or privacy email. We may disclose correspondence we hold if the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data or license Gmail data to partners.

9. Security posture

Scrappy Kin follows a zero-trust model: assume infrastructure fails, minimize the blast radius, and keep sensitive data on the user's device whenever possible. We design the product so we do not possess Gmail content that could be compelled from us; if our servers are compromised, attackers still cannot read your Gmail.

10. Your choices

For a plain-language guide to deleting local app data, disconnecting Gmail, managing subscriptions, and controlling diagnostics, see Privacy choices.

  • Disconnect Gmail inside Scrappy Kin to ask Google to revoke access and delete the saved permission from your iPhone
  • Disable Scrappy Kin's Google permission separately in Google Account settings
  • Delete your profile, email wording, send history, queue, and diagnostics through Settings > Privacy & local data
  • Ask privacy questions or request deletion of support correspondence by contacting privacy@scrappykin.com
  • Opt out of optional diagnostics entirely

11. Children's data

Scrappy Kin is intended for people 16 and older, is not directed to children under 13, and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Scrappy Kin does not ask for a birth date or verify a user's age.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post updates at scrappykin.com/privacy.html with a clear effective date and, when practical, summarize material changes inside the app.
