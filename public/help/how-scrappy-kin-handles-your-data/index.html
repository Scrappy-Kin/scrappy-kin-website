How Scrappy Kin handles your data

Scrappy Kin is designed so you do not have to hand your personal data to another company just to exercise your privacy rights. The full Gmail send-only promises live in the Privacy Policy.

In practice this means:

  • your personal profile stays on your device
  • you send requests yourself
  • Scrappy Kin does not keep a central database of your personal data
  • Gmail data does not touch Scrappy Kin servers; see the Gmail data section for full details

We try to minimize the amount of trust required.

Scrappy Kin does not:

  • read your inbox
  • collect data in the background
  • keep server-side copies of the personal data you are trying to remove

The goal is to make it easier to act while keeping control with you. If you disconnect Gmail inside Scrappy Kin or via Google account settings, the Gmail token stored on your device is deleted immediately.
