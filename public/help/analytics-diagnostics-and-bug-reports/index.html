Analytics, diagnostics, and bug reports

Scrappy Kin does not include analytics, crash reporting, or automatic bug telemetry. If support sees a bug report, it is because you chose to send it.

What the app does not do:

  • no analytics SDKs or third-party tracking tools
  • no automatic crash reporting
  • no background event tracking, session replay, or behavior analytics
  • no diagnostics uploaded to our servers

What diagnostics do exist:

  • an optional diagnostics feature in Settings
  • a small plain-text troubleshooting log stored on the device
  • diagnostics are off by default and opt-in

How sharing works:

  • Export diagnostics (plain text) copies the diagnostics text to your clipboard
  • Download diagnostics file creates a plain-text file and opens the native share sheet
  • nothing is sent in the background

The short version: no analytics, no automatic telemetry, and no automatic crash reporting. If support reads a bug report or diagnostics, it is because you deliberately exported and sent them.
