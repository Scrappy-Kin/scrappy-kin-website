How opt-out emails are sent

Scrappy Kin helps you send broker opt-out requests by email from your own Gmail account. You choose a group size, review the prepared requests, can optionally edit the email wording, and approve the send — nothing goes out without your explicit action.

The flow works like this:

  • you choose a group size: Quiet sends up to 3 emails, Steady up to 5, Fast up to 10, or All at once sends all eligible remaining emails
  • Scrappy Kin prepares the next eligible group of broker requests
  • you review the broker names and email wording and can make optional edits
  • you approve the group for sending
  • the emails go out from your account, and any reply comes back to your inbox

Scrappy Kin does not:

  • send messages without your action
  • read your inbox
  • manage your mailbox
  • store Gmail data on its servers

The point is to keep the send in your hands while making the process easier to keep up with.
