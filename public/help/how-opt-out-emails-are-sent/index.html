How opt-out emails are sent

Scrappy Kin helps you send broker opt-out requests by email from your own Gmail account.

The flow works like this:

  • Scrappy Kin shows you where to act
  • it prepares the email request
  • you review it
  • you choose to send it
  • the broker receives it from you
  • any reply comes back to your inbox

Scrappy Kin does not:

  • send messages without your action
  • read your inbox
  • manage your mailbox
  • store Gmail data on its servers

The goal is to simplify the process while keeping you in control.
