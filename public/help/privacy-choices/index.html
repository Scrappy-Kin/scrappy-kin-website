Privacy choices

Scrappy Kin keeps most app data on your device. These are the main controls you have.

Delete local app data

In Scrappy Kin, open Settings, then Privacy & local data. Choose Delete all local data.

This removes your saved profile, saved Gmail permission, email wording, sent history, send queue, and diagnostics from that iPhone. Scrappy Kin does not keep a server-side copy of your local sent history.

Deleting local app data does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Disconnect Gmail

In Scrappy Kin, open Settings, then Gmail connection, and choose Disconnect Gmail. Scrappy Kin asks Google to revoke access and deletes the saved Gmail permission from your iPhone.

You can also disable Scrappy Kin's permission from your Google Account:

https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/14012355

Google can disable the permission but cannot delete data stored on your iPhone. Use the in-app disconnect control if you also want to remove the local copy. After Google disables access, Scrappy Kin cannot send opt-out emails again unless you reconnect Gmail.

Manage your subscription

In Scrappy Kin, open Settings, then Subscription. If your subscription is active, choose Manage Subscription to open Apple's subscription management screen.

You can also manage Apple subscriptions from iPhone Settings. Apple's instructions are here:

https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428

Apple manages subscription billing. Scrappy Kin does not receive your card details, billing address, or Apple account password.

Diagnostics and bug reports

In Scrappy Kin, open Settings, then Diagnostics & app version.

Diagnostics stay on your device unless you export them. If you send diagnostics or a support email to Scrappy Kin, we see what you choose to send.

Support and privacy questions

For support, email support@scrappykin.com.

For privacy questions, email privacy@scrappykin.com.

Use the amount of detail that fits your request. If you include broker replies, screenshots, diagnostics, or other context, Scrappy Kin will receive that material in the support inbox.
