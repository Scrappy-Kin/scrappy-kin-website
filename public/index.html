Why Scrappy Kin exists

Most people have the legal right to opt out of data broker databases. In practice, though, that process is scattered across hundreds of sites and forms, which makes it hard to do on your own.

Data brokers are companies that collect, package, and sell personal information about people, often without their knowledge or consent.

Brokers bury the opt-out process on purpose. Auditing them one at a time isn't reasonable for one person, so that's the work Scrappy Kin takes on. We send brokers the same email the app prepares and record what comes back — which honor a plain email request, which push into portals or ID checks, which ignore it. Only the first group makes the list. It's short on purpose: every email is one more company seeing your information.

Scrappy Kin exists to make opt-outs something busy people can actually do while keeping you in control of your own data.

How it works

1

Send deletion requests to data brokers

Scrappy Kin starts with a deliberately short, vetted set of people search sites, marketing-list vendors, and recruiting databases that honored email requests in our testing.

Choose a group size, and Scrappy Kin prepares the next group for you to review.

2

Use the least info a broker needs to remove you

Your name, email, city, state, and the first four digits of your ZIP. Nothing more.

3

Requests go from your Gmail, not our servers

You shouldn't have to hand your personal information to one more company to get it back from others.

4

You set the pace

We do the research. You review each batch and decide what goes out, and when.

What you get

Make opt-outs something you can actually keep up with.

Your information stays with you

You don't have to hand your personal information to another company just to get started.
