4. How the Service Works

4.1 Deletion Request Process

1. You provide your personal information during account setup 2. We encrypt and securely store your information on EU servers (Germany) 3. Every 6 months, we automatically generate and send deletion requests to 800+ data brokers 4. You receive email reports showing which requests were sent and their status 5. The cycle repeats every 6 months to catch new data broker listings

4.2 Email Service Providers

  • We use third-party email service providers (ESPs) to deliver deletion requests
  • ESPs process email metadata and content as necessary for delivery
  • We select ESPs based on privacy-focused criteria and EU data residency where possible
  • ESPs are listed in our Privacy Policy

4.3 Data Retention

  • Identity data: Stored until you delete your account
  • Run history: Automatically purged after 7 days
  • Logs: Rotated every 3 days, contain no personal information
  • Email reports: Sent to you, not stored on our servers
